# Product

## Register

product

## Users
ACTRA is built for active learners, students, educators, and professionals. Their context is focused study, revision, and content creation. They need to access their learning library, complete sessions, and track progress with minimal friction.

## Product Purpose
ACTRA exists to transform passive learning materials into active practice through interactive tasks, theory linkages, and progress analytics. Success means helping users keep their practice consistent, organized, and effective in both hosted web and local desktop environments.

## Brand Personality
Modern, focused, clean, and professional. 
- **Voice**: Direct, encouraging, clear, and reassuring.
- **Tone**: Focused and premium, without unnecessary SaaS clutter or generic aesthetics.
- **Personality**: Reliable, precise, and kinetic.

## Anti-references
- Cliché SaaS templates (extreme card borders, tiny tracked all-caps eyebrows, meta-criticism copy, massive text blurs).
- Over-rounded card styling (radii above 16px).
- Inconsistent transition durations or layouts that snap awkwardly on screen size adjustments.
- High-contrast issues (e.g. muted gray text on white/near-white).

## Design Principles
1. **Kinetic Guidance**: Transitions must help orient the user when switching between authentication modes, making the app feel alive and responsive.
2. **Context Preservation**: Never disorient the user. Retain form values when returning or changing tabs.
3. **Typography First**: Clean font rendering, proportional line heights, balanced headings, and excellent readability.
4. **Adaptive Modality**: Clean transitions between local multi-profile access and hosted database logins.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict WCAG AA compliance (text contrast >= 4.5:1, interactive elements >= 3:1).
- Complete keyboard focus-ring accessibility and autocomplete support.
- Fully respected `prefers-reduced-motion` media query transitions.
